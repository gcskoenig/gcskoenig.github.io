I am a postdoctoral researcher at the *Tübingen AI Center*, working with Ulrike von Luxburg on explainable AI, causal inference, and foundation models. Before Tübingen I did my PhD with Bernd Bischl and Moritz Grosse-Wentrup at LMU Munich, the Munich Center for Machine Learning (MCML), and the University of Vienna.

My research in explainable AI (XAI) revolves around three questions: Which conclusions do existing XAI methods allow, and under what assumptions? Given a particular explanation goal, such as algorithmic recourse or learning about the data, what should explanations look like to fulfil their purpose? And, importantly, how can we estimate them efficiently and accurately? A causal perspective is central to all three: many of these questions are inherently causal, and most explanation methods perturb model inputs — a form of intervention.

Lately, I have been asking these questions in the context of tabular foundation models. If you would like to exchange ideas or collaborate, feel free to [reach out](mailto:hello@gunnarkoenig.com).
